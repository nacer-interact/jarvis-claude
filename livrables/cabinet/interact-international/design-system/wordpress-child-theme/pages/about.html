Nacer Adamou Saidou

Founder & Managing Director

Interact International was founded by Nacer Adamou Saidou, a senior executive with more than fifteen years of leadership across African institutions and markets — Cameroon, Chad, Madagascar, the Republic of Congo, and Ethiopia.

He currently serves within the African Union system, giving him a rare, current vantage point over the continent's institutional and project landscape. Across his career he has advised government ministries on multi-million-euro development programmes, delivered national infrastructure, managed large budgets, and led organisations of more than a thousand people.

He founded Interact International to become the trusted bridge between African opportunity and global capital and technology.
